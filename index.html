Each card is one way to frame the same YouTube embed. A player lazy-mounts when its card intersects the screen.

Canvas, WebGL, and WebCodecs are not in the grid. They need frames from a video element or a camera, and both are ruled out. YouTube’s own document, inside the frame, still contains their player. This page only changes the element, the embed parameters, the crop, the CSS, or the IFrame Player API.

An ad blocker can blank every YouTube frame. That is the blocker, not the method. The phone link crosses a public ntfy.sh relay tied to this session code.
